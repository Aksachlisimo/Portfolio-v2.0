LLM evaluation

Rubric-based scoring of model outputs for factuality, reasoning, instruction-following and safety.

  • Summary of failure patterns

RLHF & fine-tuning data

Preference rankings, rewritten responses and supervised examples written to hold up under reviewer QA.

  • Preference pairs with justification
  • Gold-standard rewrites

Prompt & adversarial testing

Stress-testing prompts and system instructions to find where a model breaks before your users do.

  • Reproducible failure cases
  • Prompt fixes, tested

Good for: chatbots, assistants, support bots
